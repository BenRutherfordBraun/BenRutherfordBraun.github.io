Rear Sensor Module PCB

Embedded STM32 telemetry node for real-time vehicle dynamics

Project Overview

Designed and routed a custom embedded telemetry PCB for the UBC Formula SAE Electric racecar to aggregate rear-packaging data. The module processes onboard IMU kinematics and I2C suspension telemetry, broadcasting the data over the vehicle's CAN network while independently driving the brake lights.

Key Skills & Takeaways

  • ■ Architecture Upgrades: Migrated the core architecture to an STM32H5 microcontroller and integrated a dedicated CAN transceiver to guarantee robust, high-speed data transmission to the main vehicle control unit.
  • ■ Power & Thermal Optimization: Redesigned the 5V and 3.3V buck converter topologies, maximizing power efficiency and stabilizing voltage rails for sensitive instrumentation.
  • ■ Signal Integrity: Optimized the Altium PCB layout with dedicated ground plane pours to isolate high-speed digital signals and analog sensors from high-voltage electrical noise.
  • ■ Hardware Lifecycle: Executed the physical manufacturing, rigorous bench testing, and final vehicle integration of the module, ensuring seamless integration with the custom low-voltage wiring harness.
